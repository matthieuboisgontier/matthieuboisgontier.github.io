Dr. Matthieu Boisgontier is a Professor in the Faculty of Health Sciences at the University of Ottawa. His research program integrates neuroscience, health, and psychology to improve our understanding of physical inactivity and sensorimotor control. He has been supported by NSERC, CFI, Banting Research Foundation, MITACS, and FWO. Dr Boisgontier has served on 10+ editorial boards, including Communications in Kinesiology and Peer Community In (PCI) Health & Movement Sciences. His scientific work has been featured in over 50 countries and 500 media outlets (e.g., The New York Times, The Washington Post, The Globe and Mail).
